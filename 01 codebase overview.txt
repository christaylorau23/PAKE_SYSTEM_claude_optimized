● PAKE System - Comprehensive Codebase Summary

  🎯 Executive Overview

  PAKE (Personal AI Knowledge Engineering) is a production-ready, enterprise-grade AI-powered knowledge management
  platform that provides sub-second multi-source research capabilities with advanced ML intelligence. The system
  integrates web scraping, academic databases (ArXiv), biomedical research (PubMed), and real-time analytics into a
  unified knowledge graph.
